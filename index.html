Concept 01

The Big Idea: 4 Core Parts

Before diving into cables and code, here is the whole concept in two simple sentences:

The Client-Server Cycle

"When you open a website, your device sends a request through the internet to a computer called a server. The server sends the website information back to your device."

1. Your Device

Your phone, laptop, or game console. In computer terms, this is called the Client because it starts the conversation by asking for what you want to see.

Analogy: You standing at a smoothie counter placing an order.

2. The Internet

The global network of Wi-Fi routers, underground cables, and undersea fiber lines that physically connect all computers together.

Analogy: The delivery roads and highways moving packages across town.

3. The Server

A powerful computer in a secure data center that stays on 24/7. It holds the actual website code, images, and video files.

Analogy: The kitchen and chef storing ingredients and making your order.

4. The Website

The package of files (HTML for structure, CSS for design, JavaScript for logic) your browser quickly draws onto your glass screen.

Analogy: The fresh smoothie handed back to you at the counter!

REAL INTERNET HARDWARE

Undersea & Backbone Fiber Cables

Over 95% of international internet traffic travels through heavy armored cables lying directly on the ocean floor, carrying pulses of laser light between continents!

Home Wi-Fi & Ethernet Routers

Your home router receives radio frequency signals from your phone or laptop, converts them into digital packets, and sends them out through telephone or fiber lines.
